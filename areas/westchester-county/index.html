Westchester sits across the Hudson from our Congers office. Our best-known work in the county is the Hudson River House in Croton-on-Hudson, featured in Dwell and ArchDaily.

The house opens almost entirely to the river, with large panes of glass framed in blackened steel and stone from the region. Building on a steep riverfront grade meant careful work on the foundations and drainage, and we sequenced the job to keep the site stable through every season.

Inside, rift white oak, honed stone and clean plaster run through the living spaces without a visible seam. It is the standard we bring to every Westchester project.

What have you built in Westchester?

The Hudson River House in Croton-on-Hudson, a riverfront home featured in Dwell and ArchDaily.

Can you build on a steep riverfront lot?

Yes. The Hudson River House sits on a steep grade above the river. We engineered the foundations and drainage for the slope and sequenced the job to keep the site stable year-round.

How do you price a Westchester custom home?

We price the design from schematic design and update it as the drawings develop, then take it to our trade partners for firm pricing before setting a guaranteed maximum price.
