Montana Contracting is based in Congers, and Rockland County is where we have built the longest. Since 1984 we have worked across the county, from Nyack and Palisades to New City, West Nyack and Nanuet.

Our Rockland work includes two Monte Nido treatment centers, each built inside an existing estate and finished to a residential standard. In New City we turned a Dunkin' interior in three weeks. In Nyack we built the storefront and interior for Nyack Fresh Market, and in Nanuet a full indoor family entertainment center for Catch Air. In Palisades we built a 4,900-square-foot modern ranch in cedar, ipe and stone.

Our office is in Congers, so our principals and superintendents are close to every Rockland job.
