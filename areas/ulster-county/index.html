The Saugerties Residence is a new custom home in two structures, set into the woods in Saugerties.

Vertical cedar, large spans of glass and stone set to the natural grade make up the outside. The main bath is finished in terracotta subway tile, dark stone and light cabinetry, lit by a full-width backlit mirror.

Our crews have built on remote, wooded land up and down the Hudson Valley, from the Piaule cabins above Catskill to the Germantown compound in Columbia County.

We price Ulster homes from schematic design, while the design can still change to meet the budget. One of our three principals is your contact from the first walkthrough to the final punch list.
