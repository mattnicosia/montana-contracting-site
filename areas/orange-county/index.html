Dunkin' Greenwood Lake

One lot, one road, one drive-thru that had to work. Dunkin' needed a ground-up store on a tight, high-visibility parcel on a main road in Greenwood Lake, with a full drive-thru lane fit into a site that left almost no margin for one.

The building and the drive-thru lane were planned together from the first site plan. The lane wraps close along the property line, clear of customer parking and the front entrance, with the menu board and canopy set to the grade break on that side of the lot. Stacked stone at the base and orange trim under the roofline carry the brand without crowding the site.

Inside, the counter, kitchen, and a full espresso and cold brew bar sit behind digital menu boards, finished in the dark ceiling and warm tile used across Dunkin' builds. Every utility tie-in, grading line, and curb cut was coordinated against the drive-thru lane before the foundation went in, so cars, customers, and staff move through the same tight site without crossing paths.

Tuxedo Residence

A transitional custom home set on a wooded lot in Tuxedo, built around long sightlines to the trees and a lake below. Arched windows and a two-story great room pull daylight deep into the plan.

The material palette runs light stucco and dark charcoal accent walls against warm hardwood floors, with brass and gold hardware carried through every room. A curved staircase and a coffered-ceiling foyer anchor the entry.

The kitchen centers on a black-and-white island with a marble top, flanked by a full wet bar finished in the same stone. Bathrooms throughout mix marble, stacked stone, and brass fixtures, each one built to its own material story.
