Orange County sits next to Rockland, a short drive from our Congers office. Our work here covers both sides of what we do.

In Greenwood Lake we built a ground-up Dunkin' with a full drive-thru on a tight main-road lot. The building, the lane and the parking were planned together from the first site plan, because the parcel left almost no margin.

In Tuxedo we built a transitional custom home on a wooded lot above a lake. The challenge there was the finish: light stucco, charcoal accent walls, a curved staircase, a coffered foyer and marble carried through the kitchen, bar and baths.
