Orange County borders Rockland, where our office is. We build both commercial projects and custom homes here.

In Greenwood Lake we built a Dunkin' from the ground up on a tight, high-visibility lot on a main road. The drive-thru lane wraps close to the property line, clear of the parking and the front door. Every curb cut, grading line and utility tie-in was coordinated against that lane before the foundation went in.

Tuxedo is the residential side. We built a transitional custom home on a wooded lot above a lake, with arched windows and a two-story great room that pull daylight deep into the plan.

Inside that house you walk into a coffered foyer and a curved staircase. Marble runs from the kitchen island to the wet bar, and every bathroom has its own mix of marble, stacked stone and brass.
