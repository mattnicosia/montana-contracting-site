In Germantown, Montana Contracting built a family compound of two homes on one site, featured in The New York Times and Architectural Digest.

The first is a home and workspace for an artist, where floor-to-ceiling glass brings the woods inside. The second is Meadow House, a 2,800-square-foot home in three volumes set into a sloping meadow. A curved brick wall shapes the arrival, a roof with built-in solar panels ties the volumes together, and a geothermal system heats and cools the house.

Columbia County work is a long way from our Congers office, so we plan it tightly: pricing from schematic design, a set schedule and one principal as your contact from start to finish.
