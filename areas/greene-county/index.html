Piaule is a landscape hotel on ninety acres above the town of Catskill. Garrison Architects designed it, and we built it: twenty-four cabins, a spa and a central lodge.

Vogue, Architectural Digest, Travel & Leisure and Forbes have all covered the hotel.

Each cabin is floor-to-ceiling glass, warm timber and concrete, joined to tolerances measured in millimeters. Our field teams worked with the design team the whole way to protect the mature trees and manage a sensitive grade.

We build hospitality projects and custom homes across Greene County and the Catskills, mostly on wooded land where the trees and the grade have to survive the build.
