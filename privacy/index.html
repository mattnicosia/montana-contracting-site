Privacy.

This page explains what montanacontracting.com collects and what we do with it. Montana Contracting Corp. runs this site from 173 N. Route 9W, Congers, NY 10920.

Site visits

We use Google Analytics to see how people find the site and which pages they read. It sets cookies in your browser and sends Google details such as the pages you visit, the site that sent you, your device and browser type, and your general location. We use these reports to improve the site. We do not use them to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell any data.

Google handles this data under its own policy. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services, or stop Analytics in your browser with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Blocking cookies in your browser settings works too.

Project inquiries

When you send the project form, we receive your name, email, phone number if you give it, project type and message. Our email provider delivers them to us. The site does not keep a copy. We use these details only to reply about your project, and we never sell them or share them for marketing.

Analytics records that an inquiry was sent and the project type you picked. It does not receive your name, email, phone number or message.

Questions

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email info@montanacontracting.com or call (845) 398-1778.
