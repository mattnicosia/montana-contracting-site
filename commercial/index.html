Montana Contracting builds for owners, operators and national brands. Ground-up retail. Interior buildouts. Offices. Treatment centers. Hotels and event spaces.

A lot of that work happens around a business that can't close. The fitness club in Englewood stayed open the whole time we rebuilt it in phases. Dunkin' New City reopened three weeks after we started.

In the Bronx, we gutted 26,000 square feet of raw industrial space and turned it into offices and a test kitchen for Dairyland and Chef's Warehouse. The job took all-new MEP, helical piles under the foundation and a custom interior storefront system wrapped in decorative steel.

We price the work as the design develops. At 90% plans we take it to our trade partners for firm numbers, so the guaranteed maximum price comes from the people who will build it.

For multi-family, affordable housing and larger commercial projects, we can also bring construction financing through our partner, Procida Funding.

Questions

Can you build while our business stays open?

Yes. The Englewood fitness club never closed during its phased rebuild, and each finished zone went back to the owner on a set date.

Do you build for national brands?

We have built Dunkin' stores from the ground up in Greenwood Lake and as a three-week interior job in New City, both to Dunkin's standards.

Do you offer construction financing?

Through our partner, Procida Funding, for multi-family, affordable housing, mixed-use and larger commercial work. Loan sizes run from $5M to $150M+.

Where do you build commercial projects?

Rockland, Orange, Westchester, Ulster, Greene and Columbia counties in New York, and Bergen County in New Jersey.
