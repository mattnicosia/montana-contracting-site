Our values.

Most companies frame their values and hang them on a wall. We run the entire business off ours.

These aren't a poster. They're the filter we run every decision through. We hire by them, because skill gets you an interview but values get you the job. We review our own people by them, leadership included, on a regular basis. And when someone stops living them, we have the hard conversation, no exceptions for being good at the job.

We hold our vendors, subcontractors, and clients to the same standard. The subs we put on your project, the clients we take on, all of it runs through these five values. Not words we say. The code we operate by.

1

Family First

"The homemaker has the ultimate career. All other careers exist for one purpose only - and that is to support the ultimate career."
C.S. Lewis
2

Mutual Respect

"True greatness will be achieved through the abundant mind that works selflessly - with mutual respect, for mutual benefit."
Stephen Covey
3

Collaboration

"Individual commitment to a group effort - that is what makes a team work, a company work, a society work, a civilization work."
Vince Lombardi
4

Absolute Ownership

"Responsibility equals accountability equals ownership. And a sense of ownership is the most powerful weapon a team or organization can have."
Pat Summitt
5

Attitude

"Your living is determined not so much by what life brings to you as by the attitude you bring to life; not so much by what happens to you as by the way your mind looks at what happens."
Khalil Gibran
