Montana Contracting has partnered with Procida Funding to provide construction financing for multi-family, affordable housing, and large-scale commercial projects. One conversation. One team. The build and the balance sheet, handled together.

  1. Project review. Send us the deal. We assess scope, timeline, and budget together - construction and capital under one roof.
  2. Capital structuring. Procida Funding structures the loan. Terms are built around the project, not a bank committee's template. Construction loans. Bridge financing. Mezzanine debt. Preferred equity.
  3. Execution. Montana Contracting builds it. Four decades of GC experience. The capital partner and the construction partner are in the room together from day one.
