Catskills Cabin

Category
Residential
Location
Catskills, NY
Type
Custom Home

A modern cabin in the Catskills, built around a single open living, dining, and kitchen room under a vaulted ceiling. A wood-burning stove anchors the space between two runs of tall windows facing the tree line.

The kitchen runs dark cabinetry against a white island, paired with a natural wood dining table and a loft level tucked above. Every material choice leans toward warmth: wide-plank hardwood floors, leather seating, and tile finishes that read handmade rather than manufactured.

Bathrooms carry the same restraint, fluted tile and matte black or brushed nickel fixtures against a simple white tub. The whole house is built to disappear into the woods around it.

Built with our pre-construction process.
