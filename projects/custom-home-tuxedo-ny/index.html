Tuxedo Residence

Category
Residential
Location
Tuxedo, NY
Type
Custom Home

A transitional custom home set on a wooded lot in Tuxedo, built around long sightlines to the trees and a lake below. Arched windows and a two-story great room pull daylight deep into the plan.

The material palette runs light stucco and dark charcoal accent walls against warm hardwood floors, with brass and gold hardware carried through every room. A curved staircase and a coffered-ceiling foyer anchor the entry.

The kitchen centers on a black-and-white island with a marble top, flanked by a full wet bar finished in the same stone. Bathrooms throughout mix marble, stacked stone, and brass fixtures, each one built to its own material story.

Built with our pre-construction process.
