Nyack Fresh Market

Category
Commercial
Location
Nyack, NY
Type
Grocery

A full storefront and interior buildout for an independent grocer in downtown Nyack. The signature curved sign and awning needed to read from the street, and the interior needed to move a full grocery format through checkout without a bottleneck.

Four checkout lanes sit against a branded feature wall, with a customer service counter and full deli, produce, and hot food sections laid out behind it. Exposed ceiling and track lighting keep the industrial shell honest while the finishes stay warm.

The store opened as a full-service neighborhood market, built to move volume without losing the feel of a local shop.

Built with our pre-construction process.
